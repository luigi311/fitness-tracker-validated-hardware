# Fitness Tracker for Linux

**Fitness Tracker** is an open-source app for recording running and cycling
sessions on Linux. Connect your Bluetooth sensors, follow a workout, see your
measurements in real time, and review your progress afterward. Its interface
adapts to Linux phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

This website helps you get to know the app and find hardware tested by the
community, including heart-rate monitors, foot pods, and cycling trainers.

## What you can do

- **See live sensor readings.** Connect supported Bluetooth Low Energy sensors
  and smart trainers to monitor heart rate, speed, cadence, and power as you train.
- **Record indoor and outdoor sessions.** Track running and cycling activities.
  Select GPS for outdoor speed, pace, and distance when location services are
  available on your device.
- **Follow structured workouts.** Load FIT or JSON workout files, or download
  planned workouts from Intervals.icu, and follow the targets during your session.
- **Review your workout history.** Browse recorded activities, compare sessions,
  explore graphs and summaries, and export activities as TCX files.
- **Connect your training tools.** Upload completed activities to Intervals.icu
  or sync activity records with a remote database you configure.
- **Use a Pebble companion.** View workout guidance, live sensor readings, and
  session averages on a connected Pebble watch.

Recordings are stored locally on your device, and external synchronization is
optional. You control whether location points are saved with your activities.
See the [privacy policy](@/privacy.md) for details about app data, integrations,
and website analytics.

## Get started

1. **Install the app.** Open
   [Fitness Tracker on Flathub](https://flathub.org/en/apps/com.luigi311.fitness-tracker)
   and choose **Install**. For configuration help, see the
   [app README](https://github.com/luigi311/fitness-tracker/blob/main/README.md).
2. **Check your hardware.** Browse the [device catalog](@/devices/_index.md) and
   read the notes for the features you need. Bluetooth sensor connections require
   Bluetooth Low Energy support on your Linux device.
3. **Set up a session.** Enter your personal training settings, select your
   sensors, and choose running or cycling. Start with a free session or load a
   workout; configure an external data provider when you want to sync.

## About the device catalog

Each listing records a community member's test with a specific app version,
including the verification date, working features, and any known limitations.
Use the category and support-status filters to find relevant devices. Support
labels distinguish fully supported devices from partial, experimental, and
unsupported entries; check each listing's notes for the details.

The catalog grows as people test more hardware. If you have tried a device,
[contribute your results](@/docs/contributing.md) so others can benefit from your
experience. You can submit a device report through GitHub or add an entry with a
pull request.

For problems with the app or ideas for new features, use the
[Fitness Tracker issue tracker](https://github.com/luigi311/fitness-tracker/issues).
